About

Hi, I'm Andrej.

Over a decade shipping across VR, console, PC and mobile, at Sumo Digital, Maze Theory, 22cans, PRELOADED and UNIT9. I came into production through IT infrastructure and a computer science degree, so I plan around what the build actually takes. My background in anthropology helps teams understand the why behind their work, not just the what.

I also use AI tools in my own production work and help teams adopt them without breaking their pipeline. Through my studio LudoSpark I run production training for studios and games R&D for clients including Greenpeace. I'm based in Bucharest and work in English, Romanian and Italian.

Most production work happens in the gap between what the plan says and what the build actually is. That gap is where my effort goes.
